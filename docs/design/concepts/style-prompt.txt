High-fidelity mobile app UI design screenshot, straight-on, no phone frame, no hands, 9:16, true phone scale, Android status bar. Android app for a personal AI agent called Hermes. Art direction: monochrome techwear editorial with the refinement of a premium futuristic tech brand. Plain solid near-black graphite background #0F0F10, no grid, no background lines, no texture. One type family used with conviction: a clean geometric grotesk. Screen titles in LIGHT thin weight uppercase with tight tracking at a moderate size, with a small eyebrow label above in uppercase with very wide letter spacing; body text regular around 14px in soft grey; technical data in small monospace. Soft graphite cards #1A1A1B with 20px radius and an extremely subtle sheen, no borders or very faint ones; lists use very faint hairlines instead of cards. Small icons sit in circular dark-grey wells. Primary buttons are small dark-grey filled rounded rectangles, secondary actions are plain text links, filter tags are thin outlined pills. Only special material: one small liquid polished chrome sphere representing the agent. Only color: a tiny green status dot and, where needed, a muted amber for warnings. No neon, no glow, no em dashes. Calm, airy, expensive, small refined sizes, precise alignment.
